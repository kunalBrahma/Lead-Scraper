# Lead Targets: US Small Businesses

What to scrape with the extension for Sitemaker Lab's cold email. Local SEO is the main offer.

The only real result so far is one search, `plumber in Dallas, Tx`, with 37 leads. The niche and city rankings below are judgment calls, so update them once replies come in.

## The ideal lead

Scrape and send businesses that match this:

| Trait | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Independent local service business | The owner reads the email and makes the decision |
| **Website** | Has one, with visible gaps | Website checks need something to measure |
| **Email** | On its own domain, like `owner@business.com` | These can pass verification, and work on free checks at 0 credits |
| **Google reviews** | About 10 to 500 | Established enough to pay, not so big that it has an agency |
| **Rating** | 4.0 or higher | A business with a bad reputation needs other help first |
| **Job value** | Hundreds of dollars or more per job | One extra customer can cover the cost of the service |
| **Status** | Open | Closed businesses are rejected automatically |

### Tier 3: only with a tailored pitch

Dentists, chiropractors, med spas, veterinarians, and accountants. They can pay well, but many already have marketing help, and health and legal advertising can have extra rules. Ask a professional before making claims in those fields.

### Skip for now

- **National chains and franchises:** their marketing is usually handled centrally.
- **Restaurants and small retail:** usually lower margins, and they're pitched constantly.
- **Marketing and web agencies:** they're your competitors.
- **Government, schools, hospitals, and large companies:** the wrong buyer.
- **Leads with no website and no own-domain email:** they can't be verified or measured well.

## Where to search

The send window is fixed at 9 AM to 5 PM Dallas time, on weekdays. That means:

| Time zone | Their local send time | Use? |
|---|---|---|
| **Central** | 9 AM to 5 PM | Best |
| **Eastern** | 10 AM to 6 PM | Good |
| **Mountain** | 8 AM to 4 PM | Good |
| **Pacific** | 7 AM to 3 PM | Skip for now, since it starts too early |

These hours are for winter. They shift by an hour when US daylight saving starts in March, because the window is set in UTC.

**Start with Central:**

- **Texas:** Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, Houston, San Antonio, Austin
- **Oklahoma:** Oklahoma City, Tulsa
- **Missouri and Kansas:** Kansas City, St. Louis, Wichita
- **Tennessee:** Nashville, Memphis
- **Midwest:** Minneapolis, Milwaukee, Omaha, Madison

**Then Eastern:** Atlanta, Charlotte, Raleigh, Tampa, Orlando, Columbus, Indianapolis, Louisville

**Then Mountain:** Denver, Colorado Springs, Albuquerque, Salt Lake City, Boise

Mid-sized cities and suburbs are usually better than the biggest metros, which tend to have heavier competition and more agencies. That's a judgment call, so check it against your replies.

## How to search

Use the same format as your first search, one niche and one city at a time:

```
hvac contractor in Fort Worth, Tx
electrician in Tulsa, Ok
roofing contractor in Nashville, Tn
```

- **Scrape 40 to 60 leads per search.** The review comparison needs at least 5 other leads from the same search, and more gives a fairer "typical" figure.
- **Keep one niche and city per batch.** It makes it easy to see which ones get replies.
- **Name each batch clearly,** like `hvac-fort-worth-nov`.

## How many to scrape

Don't scrape far ahead, because email results expire after 30 days and rechecking costs credits.

**From the Dallas test:**

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Leads scraped | 37 |
| Emails found | 46, or about 1.25 per lead |
| Passed verification and lead rules | 20 leads, or about 54% |

So **expect about 1 usable lead for every 2 you scrape.** This comes from one niche and one city, so it may differ elsewhere.

**Sending capacity with 2 mailboxes:**

| Period | Per day | Per week, on weekdays |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1 to 3 of warm-up | Up to 14 | Up to 70 |
| Week 4 onward, at a limit of 10 | 20 | 100 |

Warm-up and follow-ups share this limit, so real first emails will be fewer than these numbers.

**Suggested plan:**

1. **Now:** you already have 21 drafts on live. Scrape nothing more yet.
2. **Around 15 November:** scrape 1 or 2 Tier 1 searches, about 100 leads.
3. **After that:** scrape about 100 leads a week at most, and less if a queue is building up.
4. **Keep credits ready:** 100 leads use about 125 Bouncer credits, or about $1 at $8 per 1,000. You have 54 credits, which covers about 43 leads.

## Before scraping a new niche

Open **Management → Offers & Rules** and add that niche's chains to **Exclude names containing**. Check Google for current franchises in each trade. Examples:

| Niche | Names to exclude |
|---|---|
| HVAC | One Hour Heating, Aire Serv, Service Experts |
| Electricians | Mr. Electric, Mister Sparky |
| Roofers | Erie Home, LeafFilter |
| Pest control | Orkin, Terminix, Rentokil |
| Restoration | ServiceMaster, Servpro, Paul Davis |
| Garage doors | Precision Door, Overhead Door |
| Landscaping | TruGreen, Weed Man |
| Painters | CertaPro, Five Star Painting |
| Auto repair | Midas, Jiffy Lube, Firestone |

Also read the first few drafts from any new niche. Some checks were tuned on plumbers and may need adjusting:

- **Service wording:** only plumbing and HVAC currently recognize related job terms, like "water heaters" or "AC repair". Other trades may be wrongly flagged for not naming their service.
- **City wording:** only a few metro nicknames are recognized, like "DFW" and "NYC". Others may be wrongly flagged as not naming their city.

## Rules

- **Businesses only.** No personal or consumer contact lists.
- **Respect opt-outs.** The system blocks them automatically once Agent 4 sees them.
- **Don't change who you're emailing to get around a block or a bounce.**
- **Check the law for any new country.** This guide is written for US businesses only. Have a lawyer confirm your US obligations if you're unsure.
